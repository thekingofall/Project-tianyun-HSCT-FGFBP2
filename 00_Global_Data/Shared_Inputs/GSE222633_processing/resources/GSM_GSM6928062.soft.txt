!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = PBMC were isolated from up to 20ml whole blood using density gradient centrifugation. PBMC were then freshly frozen according to standard procedures and stored in liquid nitrogen. The samples were thawed and stained with 4',6- diamidino-2-phenylindole (DAPI). We sorted for alive lymphocytes using a FACSAria Fusion cell sorter (Becton Dickinson, Franklin Lakes, NJ, USA).
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Samples were treated individually, using one 10x lane per sample. Alive cells were incubated with 5 μl Human TruStain FcX™ per 1x106 cells for 10 min, then stained with nucleotide‑labeled TotalSeqCTM antibodies (Biolegend, San Diego, USA) for 30min. To avoid antibody aggregates, labeled cells were washed 3 times with 1 ml PBS/BSA with centrifugation at 300 g for 5 min. Cells were then resuspended in 50 μl PBS/BSA and counted using the Neubauer chamber. We aimed for a calculated amount of ~16.500 cells of each sample for droplet encapsulation on separate lanes of the Chromium Controller (10x Genomics, Pleasanton, CA, USA). Single-cell capturing and library construction were performed with the Chromium Next GEM Single Cell V(D)J Reagent Kit v2 (10x Genomics, Pleasanton, CA, USA) according to manufacturer’s instructions. Essentially, single-cell gel beads-in-emulsion (GEMs) are formed, each containing a single cell and 10x chemistry for cell lysis, barcoding and reverse transcription of contained RNA within each GEM. The resulting cDNA including the single-cell barcodes is then amplified using standard polymerase-chain reaction (PCR).
